About Her

She isn't loud, but she leaves a quiet impact. She's someone who notices people, tries to understand them before judging them, and carries a kind of calm that makes conversations feel easy. She loves books, writes poetry, enjoys crocheting tiny creations with patience, and finds beauty in simple things. She has a creative heart and a thoughtful mind. She's the kind of person who can tease you one moment, then ask if you've eaten the next. She has a playful side that appears when she's comfortable, and a caring side that doesn't always need many words. I've always admired the way she sees the world—not just with her eyes, but with empathy. Some conversations with her made time disappear. Some smiles made ordinary days feel special. Some silence still carried comfort. When I look at her, I don't just see a beautiful face. I see someone who inspires words I never knew I could write. She became the reason many poems were born. The reason I started noticing little moments. The reason "good morning" felt different. Maybe that's why she feels different to me. Not because she's perfect... But because she feels genuine. If I had to describe her in one sentence... "She is the kind of person who quietly becomes someone's favorite chapter without even trying." ❤️

My Heart

Dear Bangaram, Thank you for walking into my life. I don't know what tomorrow has planned for us. I only know that meeting you changed something inside me. You became the reason so many poems were born. The reason mornings felt warmer. The reason silence sometimes carried a smile. If someday you ever wonder whether you mattered... Remember this... You were never ordinary to me. You'll always remain one of the most beautiful chapters of my life. ❤️
